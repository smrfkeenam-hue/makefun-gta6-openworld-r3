**PALMIRA HEAT** — an original open-world crime game in the spirit of GTA 6, built for the make.fun gta6-01 bounty. Dana Vega, a courier new in the sun-soaked coastal city of Palmira, gets pulled into fixer Mara's crew one job at a time.

The city is yours to explore on foot and by car: traffic that drives the grid, pedestrians on the sidewalks, cars to steal, a wanted level with police who chase and try to bust you, four story missions with cash rewards, combat with pistols and fists, a HUD with a live minimap, a start screen, pause menu and a save that persists in the browser. Everything is original — city, names, characters, story, art and sound — in plain canvas 2D with WebAudio effects, no libraries and no network requests.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser.

Controls: WASD to move and drive, E to steal or exit a car, mouse to aim, click to shoot, F to punch, Space for the handbrake, Shift to run, Esc to pause, M to mute.
